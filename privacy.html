Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SE Marketing Consulting LLC, operating under the public-facing brand True Partner Tech (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, business name, and message you provide. Our hosting and email-delivery providers may also process basic technical information needed to secure and operate the service, such as IP addresses and request logs.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, discuss requested services, maintain business records, protect the website, and comply with applicable obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and retention

Cloudflare hosts the website and processes form requests. Our configured email provider delivers inquiries to us. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for business, legal, or security purposes.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you submitted or request correction or deletion, subject to records we must retain. Avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, health, or account information through the contact form.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. Questions may be sent to hello@truepartnertech.com.
